ULTRA-PERFORMANCE FEED AUTO-PLAY & VERTICAL VIDEO TRAY (ZERO-LAG ENGINE):

OBJECTIVE:
Deliver instant, 60fps buttery-smooth video playback faster than YouTube/Instagram without memory leaks or frame drops.

STRICT CONSTRAINTS:
- Do NOT alter existing full-screen video player routing, database schemas, calling, or profile logic.
- Keep the dark OLED theme intact.

ENGINEERING REQUIREMENTS:

1. HARDWARE-ACCELERATED INSTANT AUTO-PLAY:
- Implement a single shared IntersectionObserver (rootMargin: '0px', threshold: 0.5):
  * When a video is 50%+ in viewport, trigger immediate muted playback with zero delay.
  * When it falls below 50%, pause instantly and reset preload buffering to free device RAM.
  * Strictly ensure ONLY 1 video decodes/plays at any millisecond.
  * Apply CSS optimizations to video elements:
    `transform: translateZ(0); will-change: transform; backface-visibility: hidden;`
  * Add video attributes: `playsinline`, `webkit-playsinline`, `preload="metadata"`, `disablePictureInPicture="true"` (for feed previews).

2. SMART BUFFERING & MEMORY RECYCLING:
- Preload the first 2 seconds (buffer range) of the next incoming video in feed queue so it plays with 0ms delay upon scrolling.
- Automatically pause and unload off-screen video streams (`video.src = ""` or detach buffer) to prevent Android GPU/RAM choking.

3. HORIZONTAL VERTICAL VIDEO TRAY:
- Insert after every 4th or 5th regular feed post:
  * Container: `display: flex; overflow-x: auto; scroll-snap-type: x mandatory; -webkit-overflow-scrolling: touch;`.
  * Cards: Vertical 9:16 aspect ratio with rounded corners.
  * Tray preview: Autoplay only the fully visible card within the tray on horizontal swipe.
  * Tapping any card opens the exact existing video player view with current resume/playback behavior untouched.

4. SOUND CONTROLS:
- Single bottom-right floating mute/unmute icon on active feed video:
  * Global sound toggle (unmuting one feed video maintains unmuted state across feed scrolls).
