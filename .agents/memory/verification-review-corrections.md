---
name: Verification review corrections
description: Keep submitted Sports and National Award applications frozen except for evidence explicitly needed to fix a reviewer rejection.
---

When an application is pending or approved, keep its fields, documents, and video locked. When rejected or sent for correction, keep identity, country/address, award/category, and all non-evidence values locked; enable only the evidence category identified by the reviewer reason. Generic document-quality feedback may permit document replacements, but it must not unlock an introduction video. Keep private evidence previews signed and owner-scoped.

**Why:** Applicants need a safe way to fix reviewer feedback without being able to alter the identity or application details that were already reviewed.

**How to apply:** Keep the review-reason-to-evidence mapping consistent between the UI and upload/submission guards for both Sports Verification and National Award.
