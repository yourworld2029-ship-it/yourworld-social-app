---
name: Admin operations role boundary
description: YourWorld's separation between owner support administration and operational review access.
---

The support owner admin alone controls operations-admin invitations, legal/community report review, and account restrictions. Operations admins handle sports verification, National Awards, and payout review. Both roles require AAL2; each view of private payout destination details is audited.

**Why:** YourWorld requires a separate operations account without exposing copyright, grievance, or user-report administration.

**How to apply:** Keep recipient separation and server-side role checks aligned whenever admin sections or notification destinations change.
